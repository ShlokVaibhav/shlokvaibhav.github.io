Sapere Aude!

Shlok Vaibhav — founding engineer · DSP at a spacetech startup

My days are spent on signal processing and mixed-signal design — DSP for spaceborne hardware now; before this, analog design of Ethernet-SerDes and CAN at Texas Instruments.

I read electrical engineering and a master's in microelectronics at IIT Bombay, working under Prof. Bhaskaran Muralidharan on time-dependent NEGF formalism for nanostructures. I contribute to PhysLean, formalizing physics in Lean 4, and I write — mostly on philosophy, Sinology, and whatever leaves me in fear and trembling.
